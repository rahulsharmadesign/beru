# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

This project is a space for people to work on Beru without harassment. Be respectful in issues, pull requests, and discussions.

## Standards

- Assume good intent and disagree in public on the work, not the person.
- No insults, harassment, or sexualized language.
- No publishing others’ private information.
- Do not use this project to attack people or ship malware.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/rahulsharmadesign/beru/security/advisories/new) if they involve safety or abuse, or by opening an issue labeled `conduct` for public community matters.

Maintainers may warn, limit, or ban anyone who breaks this standard.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issue tracker, discussions, and any official Beru presence.
